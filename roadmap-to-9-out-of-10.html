Vibed Roadmap

How to go from "good idea" to "must-use platform"

1 Solve the Cold Start

The problem: You need content before you have an audience, but creators won't create for an empty room.

The Solution: Be the Content

  • Film 20 founders yourself — Interview them, edit the clips, structure their journey for them. Don't wait for them to figure it out.
  • Partner with accelerators — YC, Techstars, On Deck, Antler. Offer to document their cohort for free. They get content; you get founders.
  • Pay 5 founders with existing audiences — Find founders with 10K+ Twitter/YouTube followers. Pay them to cross-post their Vibed clips.
  • Create a "Founding Founders" program — First 50 founders get lifetime Pro, personal onboarding, featured placement.

You need 20 compelling journeys before you launch publicly. Quality over quantity.

2 Make Video Creation 10x Easier

Current friction: Record → Edit → Upload → Write description → Publish

What it should be: Open app → Answer prompt → Done (30 seconds)

Features to Build

Feature Why It Matters
One-tap mobile recording Capture moments as they happen
Daily/weekly prompts "What did you ship this week?" removes decision fatigue
Auto-captions 80% of video is watched muted
AI clip extraction Record 5 min, AI picks the best 60 seconds
Voice-only mode For camera-shy founders, audio with waveform visual
Auto-compiled weekly digest 7 days of clips become one "Week 12 Update"

Steal from Loom, not YouTube. Founders don't have time for production. Make it effortless.

3 Make Promises Your Killer Feature

Promises exist in Vibed. They should be the reason founders come.

Promise 2.0 Features

  • Promise templates: "Ship MVP by [date]", "Get 10 paying customers by [date]", "Launch on Product Hunt by [date]"
  • Stakes: Founders put $50-500 on the line. If they fail, it goes to charity. If they succeed, they get a badge.
  • Auto-verification: Connect GitHub (commits), Stripe (revenue), App Store (downloads) to verify promises automatically
  • Leaderboard: "Founders who kept 90%+ of promises" — trust signal for followers
  • Notifications: Followers get notified 24 hours before a promise is due. Creates anticipation.
  • Promise history: Visual timeline of kept vs broken promises. Transparency builds trust.

This becomes "accountability-as-a-service." Founders pay for the discipline, not the platform.

4 Create "Early Follower" Status

Make following early feel like an investment.

  • Permanent badge: "Followed since Week 1" visible on your profile forever
  • Exclusive access: Early followers get behind-the-scenes updates, direct Q&A
  • Bragging rights: "I followed [now-famous founder] when they had 12 followers"
  • Early follower wall: On each venture page, show the first 100 followers prominently
  • Notifications: "A venture you followed early just hit 10K followers!"

FOMO drives growth. People want to discover the next big thing before everyone else.

5 Structured Comparisons

Your 16 segments enable something no competitor can do: comparing journeys.

Content Formats to Create

  • "How 10 founders handled their first sale" — Same segment, different founders, compiled view
  • "Pivot stories: Before and after" — Side-by-side comparison of original idea vs current product
  • "Week 4 vs Week 40" — Same founder, same question, different stages. See the growth.
  • "The spark that started it" — Collection of "spark" segments from successful ventures
  • Industry comparisons: "How SaaS founders vs hardware founders approach beta"

Turn individual journeys into collective wisdom. This is your content moat.

6 Founder-Pays Monetization

Don't monetize the audience. Monetize the founders.

Tier Price Features
Free $0 1 venture, basic analytics, community features
Pro $19/mo Unlimited ventures, detailed analytics, export tools, priority support
Team $49/mo Multiple founders, shared dashboard, team permissions

Premium Add-Ons

  • Verified founder badge: $99 one-time — identity verification, extra trust signal
  • Analytics dashboard: Who watched, for how long, where they dropped off, follower demographics
  • Export tools: Download your journey as a documentary video, PDF book, or portfolio page
  • Custom domain: yourname.vibed.io
  • API access: Pull your data into other tools

7 Distribution Built-In

Every clip should spread Vibed's reach.

  • Auto-generate social versions: Square for Instagram, vertical for TikTok/Reels, horizontal for YouTube/Twitter
  • Watermarked clips: Subtle "vibed.io/founder" watermark drives traffic back
  • Weekly email digest: Followers get a curated email with updates from ventures they follow
  • RSS feed: Each venture has a feed for podcast apps, Feedly, etc.
  • Embeddable player: Founders can embed their Vibed journey on their own website
  • Share cards: Beautiful preview cards when sharing to Twitter/LinkedIn/Slack

Vibed should make founders more visible everywhere, not just on Vibed.

8 Quality Over Quantity

Curate ruthlessly. 10 great journeys beat 1,000 dead profiles.

  • Application process: Featured ventures must apply and be approved
  • Editor's picks: Human-curated "ventures worth watching" with real editorial judgment
  • Activity requirements: Ventures that don't update for 30 days get moved to "archive"
  • Consistency rewards: Founders who update weekly get boosted in discovery
  • Quality signals: Promise kept rate, update frequency, follower engagement — all visible
"The platform where founders make public promises, document progress in 30-second videos, and build an audience who followed them before they were famous."

The 9/10 Test

You'll know you've reached 9/10 when:

  • Founders say "I use Vibed for accountability" not "I post on Vibed"
  • Followers brag about who they discovered early
  • Clips from Vibed show up on Twitter/LinkedIn organically
  • YC founders ask to be featured, not the other way around
  • Someone writes "The best startup documentaries are on Vibed"
