# Vibed Intro Video — Individual Scenes for AI Video Generation

Each scene is designed to be generated independently and stitched together.
Total video: 60 seconds

---

## WHAT THIS VIDEO IS ABOUT

Vibed is a platform where founders document their startup journey from day one — the real story, not the polished version.

**The Call to Action:**
Watch founders build. Learn from their journey. Then start your own.

---

## THE FOUR PILLARS

| Pillar | Tagline |
|--------|---------|
| **Be curious** | "Discover the ideas people are building — before the headlines, before the hype." |
| **Be inspired** | "Follow their journey from week one. The wins. The struggles. Everything." |
| **Be ready** | "Learn from the moments that almost broke them — and the breakthroughs that changed everything." |
| **Be next** | "Start your venture. Document your journey. We want to follow you." |

---

## PRODUCTION NOTE

No legible text should appear in any scene visuals. All writing, screens, and whiteboards should be filmed from angles or distances that make text illegible.
