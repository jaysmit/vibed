# Vibed Intro Video — Full Production Script

Duration: 60 seconds
Aspect Ratio: 16:9 (landscape)
Resolution: 4K

---

## WHAT THIS VIDEO IS ABOUT

Vibed is a platform where founders document their startup journey from day one — the real story, not the polished version. This intro video explains the concept to new visitors and invites them to either follow founders or start their own venture.

**The Call to Action:**
Watch founders build. Learn from their journey. Then start your own.

---

## THE FOUR PILLARS

The video is structured around four pillars, each with its own message:

### 1. BE CURIOUS
**Tagline:** "Discover the ideas people are building — before the headlines, before the hype."

What it means: Vibed lets you find founders working on real things right now. Not success stories told in hindsight, but ventures in progress. You get to see what's being built before anyone else knows about it.

---

### 2. BE INSPIRED
**Tagline:** "Follow their journey from week one. The wins. The struggles. Everything."

What it means: Unlike polished case studies, Vibed shows the real timeline. Founders post weekly updates — what worked, what didn't, what they learned. You follow along as it actually happens.

---

### 3. BE READY
**Tagline:** "Learn from the moments that almost broke them — and the breakthroughs that changed everything."

What it means: The most valuable lessons come from struggles, not just wins. On Vibed, founders share their failures openly. You learn what to expect, what to avoid, and how others pushed through.

---

### 4. BE NEXT
**Tagline:** "Start your venture. Document your journey. We want to follow you."

What it means: Vibed isn't just for watching — it's for doing. When you're ready to build something, start documenting it. No audience required. Just honesty.

---

## VISUAL STYLE GUIDE

### Colour Palette
- Primary background tones: warm whites, soft greys, natural wood, concrete
- Accent colour: Vibed green (#05CE78) — used sparingly on text
- Skin tones: warm and natural, not overly graded
- Overall grade: slightly desaturated with lifted blacks, film-like

### Lighting
- Natural light preferred — window light, golden hour, overcast soft light
- Practicals (desk lamps, screen glow) for night scenes
- Avoid harsh shadows or overly stylised lighting

### Camera Feel
- Handheld with subtle movement — human, not robotic
- Mix of intimate close-ups and wider environmental shots
- Occasional lens flares from windows/screens (subtle)

### Footage Style
- Blend of cinematic B-roll and authentic phone-recorded clips
- Real faces, diverse founders (age, gender, ethnicity, location)
- Workspaces: garages, bedrooms, cafes, small offices
